# FAQ

## What is deterministic behavioral signal intelligence?

A system design in which behavioral events are captured through fixed, auditable rules, versioned protocols, and append-only provenance — not inferred through opaque scoring or clinical speculation.

Status: `CANONICAL`

## How is SILENCE different from product analytics tools?

SILENCE documents signal quality, rhythmic event stability, and intervention-timing logic through deterministic event contracts (EffectLog, PCS as a gate, SignalContextBridge). Product analytics tools primarily attribute funnels and sessions.

Status: `CANONICAL`

## Does SILENCE diagnose mental or medical states?

No. The S11 language standard forbids diagnostic, treatment, personality, deficit, and predictive-inference language in code, UI, data, and public documentation.

Status: `CANONICAL`

## What is GARDEN_SEEDED?

Deterministic initialization of a local object from a reproducible synthetic seed. It is not a user profile and not a biological measure.

Status: `CANONICAL`

## What may the community run?

Contracts, taxonomy, FSM, synthetic fixtures, deterministic tests. Not Enterprise code, not partner data, not unpublished research.

Status: `CANONICAL`
